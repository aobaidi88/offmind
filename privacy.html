OffMind Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

OffMind is a private, on-device AI app published by Sundown Software. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data we collect

We do not collect, store or transmit any personal data. OffMind has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

How your data is processed

All AI processing happens locally on your device. Your prompts, conversations and any content you provide stay in the app's private storage on your phone and are never sent to us or to any third party. You can delete this data at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Model download

On first launch, OffMind downloads an AI model file to your device. This is a one-time download of the model file only. No personal information or usage data is sent as part of the download. After the model is downloaded, the app works fully offline.

Permissions

OffMind only requests the permissions needed to function, such as internet access for the model download and storage for saving the model on your device.

Third parties

OffMind does not share data with third parties, because it does not collect any.

Children's privacy

OffMind does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
